Discover your political orientation tailored specifically to the European ideological landscape.

What is EuropeanValues?

EuropeanValues is a political quiz based on AmericanValues 2 (which is based on 8values), but tailored specifically to the political climate, treaties, and socio-economic systems of Europe. You will be presented with a statement, and you will answer with your opinion from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree, with each answer slightly affecting your scores.

There are questions in the test.

What are the European Values?

There are five independent axes, each contrasting two competing perspectives:

Europeanism

Favors deeper European integration, supranational institutions, pooled sovereignty, and moving toward a federal Europe.

Euroscepticism

Advocates for national sovereignty, intergovernmental subsidiarity, border controls, and returning power to nation-states.

Socialism

Believes in universal welfare, labor protections, progressive taxation, and strategic public ownership of infrastructure.

Liberalism

Supports open single-market competition, labor deregulation, fiscal discipline, and privatization of state monopolies.

Progressivism

Favors modern social reforms, secularism, cultural pluralism, and individual self-determination.

Conservatism

Upholds traditional social institutions, Christian-democratic heritage, and cultural preservation.

Ecologism

Prioritizes emissions reductions, environmental mandates, biodiversity, and an accelerated green energy transition.

Productivism

Prioritizes industrial output, energy affordability, farmer protections, and economic growth over green restrictions.

Atlanticism

Prioritizes close alignment with the United States and the NATO collective defense framework.

Continentalism

Advocates for European strategic autonomy, independent military capability, and an autonomous diplomatic line.

Sanctuary Europe

Prioritizes universal human rights, generous asylum protections, state-supported maritime search-and-rescue, and legal migration corridors.

Fortress Europe

Prioritizes hardened external border walls, Frontex interdiction, third-country offshore processing centers, and rapid deportations.
